# Lawhive

> Lawhive is a UK consumer legal-services marketplace that connects people and small businesses with regulated solicitors online at fixed, transparent prices. Clients answer intake questions on the site, get a quote, and are matched with a vetted lawyer who runs the matter through Lawhive's platform. Lawhive Ltd is not a law firm; legal work is delivered by its network, including affiliate firm Lawhive Legal Ltd (SRA ID 8003766). Lawhive publishes no public developer API, SDK, or API documentation as of this writing.
